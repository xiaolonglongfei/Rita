Rovi must not present itself as Westchester-only; the user said, "We will expand beyond Westchester."

**Why:** The product will serve more regions, so marketing and legal descriptions must not imply a geographic service restriction.

**How to apply:** Use region-neutral product descriptions. Preserve genuine location data and New York governing-law language; those are not claims that the service is Westchester-only.

Review ratings and comments stay anonymous, but a claimed instructor sees the student's account name and session date, time, and location privately to confirm the session. Names are never shown alongside published reviews or ratings.

**Why:** The user explicitly distinguished private session confirmation from public review anonymity and asked to stop promising that identities are never disclosed to instructors.

**How to apply:** Keep public reviews anonymous and instructor verification limited to session details. Do not add rating or comment fields to instructor session lists or verification emails.

Public copy must not claim reviews or instructors are generally "verified." Use "Lesson confirmed" only to mean that the instructor confirmed the lesson took place, not that the rating or comments were verified.

**Why:** The user says only instructors who have claimed their profile are asked to confirm lessons; confirmation is not a universal property of reviews.

**How to apply:** Use "Not yet confirmed" for the other state. Keep accurate identity/login-link verification statements and legal disclaimers about not verifying opinions; do not rename internal fields or change confirmation, scoring, or giveaway logic to match the wording.
