Context: On /win, after a successful signup the user immediately sees
"You're entered! We'll email you if you win." That's wrong. The point of the
campaign is to get reviews, and the page's own "How to enter" steps list the
review (step 2) before "You're entered" (step 3). A user is only entered once
they have at least one qualifying review.

- Opt-in: signup through /win with rules accepted -> giveaway_opt_in_at
  (keep as is; it is the consent timestamp).
- Qualifying review: a review by that user, created at/after
  giveaway_opt_in_at and within the Promotion Period (Sept 21 - Dec 31,
  2026), that was successfully saved, i.e. it passed the Layer 1 content
  filter. Blocked submissions never count.
- Entered = at least one qualifying review. No new column is needed; derive
  this from the reviews table.

  after instructor-side session verification, do NOT silently require that
